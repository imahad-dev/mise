# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

- **Guests** at a restaurant table or counter. They scan a QR code on their own phone, often in dim light, often with one hand, and may never have used the app before. Their job is to choose, order, ask for help and pay without thinking about software.
- **Staff and kitchen.** They approve first orders, open tables, read tickets and settle bills during a busy service.
- **Owners.** They read revenue, margin and menu reports, and edit the menu.

## Product Purpose

Mise is a build-for-you studio product: complete systems that raise a restaurant's revenue (ordering, digital menus, kitchen display, bills, analytics), delivered to each restaurant under that restaurant's own brand. Success is a guest finding every step easy, and an owner earning more from the same tables.

## Positioning

Each venue gets its own identity on one safe engine. The guest sees the restaurant, not a software vendor. The money, allergen and kitchen rules underneath are identical everywhere.

## Operating Context

Four venues, each in its own country's currency and under its own tax rules:

- Angeethi House, Lahore (PKR)
- Kehwa & Crumb, Islamabad (PKR)
- Noor & Za'atar, Dubai (AED, 5% VAT included in prices)
- Tikka Town, Manchester (GBP, 20% VAT included in prices, optional service charge)

The user treats these venues as real. Mise sets every standard for them: logo, pictures, colours and type.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Mise sets each brand. Owners add photos. Owner app and kitchen display keep Mise's own look.
- Prices, allergen chips and warnings, the pay sheet, table status and Call waiter stay consistent and legible on every venue.
- Allergen status is deterministic server code. Guests never settle bills.
- Demo bills never imitate a tax invoice.
- Free tools and free fonts only.
- Interface language is English for now. A venue's brand name may be in any script.

## Brand Commitments

Each venue has a two-colour palette chosen by the user:

- Angeethi House: Wine Red #7F011F, Light Sand #F5EBDD
- Kehwa & Crumb: Forest #064734, Leaf #E0FFC2
- Noor & Za'atar: Royal Blue #014BAA, Light Cream #F8F3F0
- Tikka Town: Orange #EC5E27, Milk #FDFDF1

Plain restaurant words, no technical terms. Mise's own identity comes later.

## Evidence on Hand

No real photographs, customers, reviews or founding dates exist for these venues. Never invent them ("Est. 1962", awards, quotes). Stock dish photos are allowed with credits.

## Product Principles

- Guests must feel easy at every step.
- No margin of error in payment or any workflow; test it from the real world.
- A venue's character never reduces the legibility of price, allergens or payment.
- Everything is built from what truly belongs to that venue.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Text contrast of at least 4.5:1, touch targets of at least 44px, colour never the only signal, reduced motion respected.
