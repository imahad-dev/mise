## Desi family restaurant: categories, portion conventions, PKR prices

### Takeaway
Mid-range desi family restaurants sell karahi by weight (½ kg or 1 kg) or as Half/Full, BBQ by the piece or plate, and breads per piece. In 2025–26 chicken karahi is about Rs 2,000–2,200 per kg and mutton karahi about Rs 4,500–6,600 per kg/full. Breads are Rs 50–340, single-serve daal and biryani are Rs 300–600, and a chicken tikka piece is about Rs 840 at a branded chain. Lassi ranges from Rs 700 at a premium mutton house down to much less at budget places.

## Trendy café / coffee spot: categories, sizes, add-ons, PKR prices

At mid-range trendy cafés in Islamabad, Lahore and Karachi in 2026, an Americano costs about Rs 350–680 and a cappuccino or latte about Rs 500–800. Spanish and flavoured lattes cost Rs 600–1,315. ## Food cost % / margins in Pakistan

### Takeaway
I found no reliable Pakistan-specific food-cost benchmark. International coffee-shop benchmarks put beverage COGS at about 25–35% of price (latte about 30%). Pakistani raw-meat prices sit far below menu karahi prices (mutton raw Rs 2,200–3,000/kg vs Rs 4,500–6,600/kg on menus), which points to meat-dish food cost of roughly 35–50% before oil, spices and gas.

### Cited Findings
- Coffee shop COGS 25–35% of revenue; net margin 10–25% — [Restaurant Warehouse](https://therestaurantwarehouse.com/blogs/restaurant-equipment/coffee-shop-profit-margin)
- Specialty-coffee COGS 30–40%; ingredients alone 15–25% — [Zest Coffee, 2023](https://www.zestcoffee.com.au/2023/04/21/boosting-your-cafe-profitability/)
- Latte example: $4.00 price, $1.20 ingredients, 30% — [Per Diem](https://www.tryperdiem.com/chapters-collection/menu-math-made-simple); 16 oz latte cost $0.85–1.35 — [Bellwether](https://bellwethercoffee.com/blog/coffee-shop-profit-margins)
- Pakistan restaurant KPI vendor blogs give impossible numbers (food cost 120–140% of sales, gross margin 860%). Do not use — [financialmodelslab](https://financialmodelslab.com/blogs/kpi-metrics/pakistani-restaurant)
- Average full-service menu prices in Pakistan fell 36.52% between 2023 and H1 2024 (all-channel −7.82%) — [Verdict Foodservice](https://www.verdictfoodservice.com/data-insights/pakistan-fsr-menu-analysis/)
- Raw mutton Rs 2,200–3,000/kg and chicken Rs 550–1,200/kg in 2025 — [Express Tribune](https://tribune.com.pk/story/2519896/uncomfortable-prices-break-back-of-peshawarites), [ProPakistani](https://propakistani.pk/2025/01/13/chicken-beef-and-mutton-are-selling-at-double-the-official-price-in-lahore/)

### Inferences
- Suggested seed food-cost % for demo analytics: mutton dishes 40–48%; chicken karahi/handi 32–40%; BBQ 33–40%; biryani/pulao 28–35%; daal/sabzi 18–25%; breads 12–20%; raita/salad 15–20%; desserts 22–30%; lassi 18–25%; soft drinks/water 45–60% (bought in at near-retail); chai 12–18%. Café: espresso drinks 20–30%; drinks with alt milk or imported syrups 30–38%; frappés/shakes 25–32%; bakery 30–40%; sandwiches 32–40%; cheesecake 30–38%. ## Common allergens and Pakistani allergen-labelling practice

### Takeaway
Allergen labelling on Pakistani restaurant menus is generally absent. Punjab Food Authority ordered nutrition/allergen info in 2023, but the order covered mainly 4–5 star hotels and clubs, and current enforcement is unconfirmed.

### Cited Findings
- PFA (2023) order: allergen and nutrition info on the standard menus of hotels, 4- and 5-star hotels and recreation clubs; also reported as "all restaurants and hotels to mention nutrition information" — [ProPakistani, 2023-07-21](https://propakistani.pk/2023/07/21/punjab-restaurants-and-hotels-ordered-to-list-nutrition-information-on-food-items/amp/)
- PFA Technical Wing handles labelling of pre-packed foods; no restaurant-menu rule is shown — [pfa.gop.pk](https://pfa.gop.pk/wings/technical/)
- India's FSSAI comparator: chains with 10+ outlets must show allergens (gluten cereals, crustaceans, milk, egg, fish, nuts, soy) — [Food Business MEA](https://www.foodbusinessmea.com/fssai-new-menu-labelling-laws-targets-food-restaurants/)

### Inferences (dish-level allergen mapping from standard recipes; not sourced, verify per recipe)
- Dairy: karahi (often finished with butter/cream/yoghurt), handi/makhni, malai boti, reshmi kebab (cream/cheese), raita, lassi, kheer, gajar halwa, palak paneer, doodh patti; almost all café milk drinks, cheesecake, croissants (butter).
- Gluten: all naan/roti/paratha, samosa, fish pakora (besan + sometimes flour), bakery, sandwiches, burgers, brownies, pancakes, waffles.
- Egg: naan (often egg-washed or in dough), reshmi kebab, fish pakora batter, all cakes/brownies/pancakes/French toast, mayo in sandwiches.
- Nuts: kheer, gajar halwa (almond/pistachio), some handi/korma (cashew or almond paste), pistachio drinks, Nutella items (hazelnut), almond croissant.
- Sesame: buns, some naan (til topping).
- Mustard: achari dishes and achari naan (mustard oil and seeds); some seekh/tikka marinades use mustard oil.
- Fish: fish pakora. Soy: rarely, in some sauces or bakery.

## Peak hours, dayparts, weekends and Ramadan

### Takeaway
Dinner is the dominant daypart and runs late. Cities traditionally stayed open until about 11pm or later, and 2026 energy-saving orders cap restaurant dine-in at 11pm (Islamabad, Balochistan) or a 7–11:30pm dinner window (Sindh), with delivery and takeaway exempt. In Ramadan, iftar is the main ordering window and sehri demand is weaker. Total daily transactions historically dip during Ramadan, while delivery ordering grows.

### Cited Findings
- Islamabad (June 2026): restaurants may operate until 11pm; takeaway and delivery exempt; shops close at 9pm — [ProPakistani, 2026-06-03](https://propakistani.pk/2026/06/03/islamabad-revises-business-timings-again/amp/)
- Sindh (April 2026): hotels, restaurants and food outlets open 7pm–11:30pm (dinner timings); Karachi shops close at 9pm — [ProPakistani, 2026-04-10](https://propakistani.pk/2026/04/10/sindh-announces-new-timings-for-markets-restaurants-and-wedding-halls/amp/); [Arab News](https://www.arabnews.pk/pakistan/pakistans-sindh-orders-early-closure-of-businesses-to-conserve-energy-2639441)
- Balochistan (Sept 2026): restaurants and cafés close by 11pm; delivery exempt — [ARY News](https://arynews.tv/new-market-restaurant-timings-announced-in-balochistan)
- Markets in major cities traditionally remain open until about 11pm or later (2023) — [Aaj News](https://english.aaj.tv/news/30308715)
- Ramadan: riders face tight iftar/suhoor timing (Islamabad, March 2026) — [Arab News](https://www.arabnews.com/node/2594113/amp)
- 2015 data: Ramadan orders on foodpanda.pk up almost 100% YoY, though daily transactions usually fall by more than 10% in Ramazan compared with other months; homemade food early in the month, shifting to fast food later — [Express Tribune](https://tribune.com.pk/story/922867/age-of-tech-appetite-for-online-food-orders-continues-to-grow)
- 2010 Lahore: ordering trend limited to iftar offers, little sehri ordering — [Express Tribune](https://tribune.com.pk/story/43884/entertainment)

### Inferences
- Demo seed shape. Desi restaurant: lunch 1–3:30pm (smaller); dinner 8–11pm peak, max 9–10:30pm; Fri–Sun dinner 1.4–1.8x weekdays. Café: late-morning brunch 11am–1pm on weekends; afternoon 4–6pm; evening/late peak 8–11pm (students, dates, after-dinner coffee/dessert). Ramadan: near-zero lunch at desi places; spike in the 30–60 min around iftar (about 6–7:30pm depending on season) with iftar platters; second, smaller wave 10pm–midnight; sehri 2–4am only at select venues. Cafés see a post-taraweeh rush (about 10pm–1am) where hours allow. Under 2026 closing rules, dine-in ends at 11pm and late demand shifts to delivery.
