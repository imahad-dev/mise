# Pakistan restaurant ordering, POS and KDS: market and competitors (researched 2026-10-22)

Source caveat for the whole file: most pricing and feature claims come from vendor websites or vendor-written comparison and SEO blogs (many of them competitors of Foodpanda or of each other). Independent data (surveys, market reports) is scarce. Treat vendor numbers as claims, not verified facts.

## Which restaurant POS / ordering systems are used in Pakistan, and what do they offer?

### Takeaway
The market is crowded and cheap. Local cloud POS vendors (Oscar, CORN POS, Blink, Mint POS, Moneypex, eHisabKitab, Granet Pro and others) all sell FBR/PRA/SRB tax integration as a core feature, mostly at PKR 1,500–17,500 per branch per month. Separate 0%-commission ordering and QR-menu players (OnlineOrder.pk, Indolj, EMenu, Digital QR Menue, Menuthere) undercut Foodpanda. KDS, QR table ordering and Foodpanda sync are now common claims. Oscar gives its POS away free and earns from card processing. Analytics is marketed as plain reporting or "BI dashboards", not as an explained or narrative product.

### Inferences

- Oscar's free POS funded by card fees, with KDS, QR, Foodpanda sync and offline mode, is the most direct threat to a new ordering + KDS + analytics product.
- Most players bundle many features. Vendors pitch analytics as "reports" or "BI dashboards". None was seen offering plain-language, owner-facing explanations of revenue, which could be a positioning gap. This is a weak signal from marketing pages only.
- Allergen handling, and EN/IT language support, did not appear anywhere. Local menus are English and Urdu, so Italian is irrelevant for Pakistan; Urdu support would matter.

## How common is QR menu / QR table ordering, and how do guests feel about it?

### Takeaway
QR menus and QR table ordering are widely offered by Pakistani vendors (most POS and ordering vendors list them), and one QR player claims 1,000+ Pakistani restaurants. No Pakistan-specific adoption rate or guest-attitude survey was found. The only survey data is from the US and is negative toward QR-only menus.

### Cited Findings
- Digital QR Menue claims 1,000+ restaurants in Pakistan using its WhatsApp-based QR ordering. — [digitalmenue.com](https://www.digitalmenue.com/)
- QR table ordering is listed for Blink, Indolj, Menuthere, Oscar, CORN POS, EMenu, FoodChow and Odoo in a 2026 Pakistan comparison. — [Menuthere list](https://menuthere.com/best-online-ordering-systems/pakistan)
- A Pakistani marketing guide says white-label ordering systems are "proliferating at low monthly cost" and recommends table QR codes and WhatsApp ordering to move repeat orders off delivery apps. — [salmangul.com](https://salmangul.com/food-restaurant-qsr-marketing-pakistan/)
- US data, not Pakistan: a Toast survey of 850 adults found 81% prefer a physical menu and only 1% prefer QR codes over other formats. — [Toast](https://pos.toasttab.com/blog/on-the-line/qr-code-menu-insights)

### Inferences
- Pakistani table-service norms (plentiful, low-cost waitstaff) likely reduce the labour-saving pitch of QR ordering compared with Western markets. This is unverified. Positioning QR ordering as a speed, accuracy and upsell tool alongside waiters (a hybrid) may land better than "replace the waiter".
- WhatsApp is the dominant ordering habit pattern for small outlets per vendor positioning.

### Gaps
- No Pakistan survey on QR menu usage or guest attitudes; no adoption rate post-COVID.

## Aggregators: Foodpanda commission, market share, pain points, and own ordering channels

### Takeaway
Foodpanda dominates Pakistani food delivery. It turned profitable in 2024 and aims to double GMV in three years. Commonly cited commissions are 25–35% plus a subscription fee, tax on the commission and funded promotions. These figures come from commission-free competitors; no official rate card was found. A clear "own channel" vendor segment exists (0% commission, roughly PKR 5,000/month).

### Cited Findings
- Foodpanda Pakistan's first annual profit: net income €11.2M in 2024, with negative equity of €137.1M. — [TechJuice](https://www.techjuice.pk/foodpanda-pakistans-e11-2-million-profit-milestone-or-mirage/)
- Aims to double Pakistan GMV in three years (August 2025). — [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2025-08-07/foodpanda-aims-to-double-pakistan-business-in-three-years)
- Handles up to 20% of the total food business for its restaurant partners. — [Arab News](https://arabnews.com/node/2612269/amp)
- CEO Muntaqa Peracha: pandapro is about 30% of orders, Pakistan is Foodpanda's largest grocery market globally, and groceries are almost one-third of its Pakistan business. — [Business Recorder](https://www.brecorder.com/news/40399530/interview-with-muntaqa-peracha-ceo-foodpanda-pakistan-pakistan-is-now-foodpandas-largest-grocery-market-globally)
- A LUMS study commissioned by Foodpanda estimated a USD 1.2B economic contribution in FY2023-24 with 13,000+ partner restaurants (company-promoted). — [PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/apac/news-releases/independent-study-reveals-usd-1-2-billion-economic-impact-of-foodpanda-in-pakistan-for-2023-2024--302530193.html); [QSR Media](https://qsrmedia.com/food-services/news/foodpanda-injects-12b-pakistans-economy-in-2023-2024)
- An older Dawn piece cites about 90% market share (company-side, older). — [Dawn](https://www.dawn.com/news/1616450)
- Commission is cited at 25–35% depending on tier and visibility. Extras cited: a subscription of about PKR 1,860, roughly 16% tax on the commission, payment charges, about 5% for premium placement, and funded promotions; all-in can exceed 35–40%. All of these sources are competing vendors. — [letmenu](https://letmenu.com/blog/foodpanda-commission-rate-pakistan); [digiteazy](https://digiteazy.com/foodpanda-commission-pakistan/); [WoBooks](https://wobooks.com/compare/foodpanda)
- A 2020 Karachi boycott by about 200 restaurants over commission rising from 18% to 35% is cited by vendor blogs. I could not verify it against news sources. — [digiteazy](https://digiteazy.com/foodpanda-commission-pakistan/)
- Cheetay, a former Foodpanda rival, was reported "on the brink of shutting down" after halving staff (Profit, 2024). CB Insights lists a 2024 shutdown or major downturn. Another source says it pivoted to quick commerce. Current status is unconfirmed. — [Profit](https://profit.pakistantoday.com.pk/?p=160497); [CB Insights](https://www.cbinsights.com/company/cheetay); [Digital Pakistan](https://digitalpakistan.pk/pakistan-food-delivery-apps)
- Own-channel offers: OnlineOrder.pk Rs 4,999/month at 0% commission; Indolj Rs 4,999 + 1%; Blink website $50/branch/month. A vendor claims restaurants move 30–60% of repeat orders to their own site within 6–12 months (unverified). — [OnlineOrder.pk](https://onlineorder.pk/onlineorder-pk-vs-foodpanda/); [Menuthere list](https://menuthere.com/best-online-ordering-systems/pakistan)

### Inferences
- Effectively a Foodpanda near-monopoly in delivery, so restaurants cannot leave it. A product should integrate with or coexist alongside Foodpanda (Oscar and Blink already sync), not replace it.
- Analytics that compare aggregator net margin against own-channel and dine-in margin would speak to a well-known pain point.

### Gaps
- No official Foodpanda commission rate card; no independent 2025–26 market-share figures versus other apps.

## Market size: restaurants, café boom, chains versus independents

### Takeaway
Hard numbers are old and inconsistent: 75,000–90,000 restaurants nationally (Dawn, older), and 13,000–20,000+ on Foodpanda. The café boom in Lahore, Karachi and Islamabad is documented only qualitatively. Large local QSR chains (Cheezious, OPTP, Crumble) are already Blink customers.

### Cited Findings
- Up to 90,000 restaurants nationally in one Dawn piece. A Foodpanda director quoted in Dawn said no more than 75,000, including dhabas. Both are undated relative to 2025. — [Dawn](https://www.dawn.com/news/1616450); [Dawn](https://www.dawn.com/news/1652493)
- Foodpanda partners: more than 20,000 registered (older), 13,000+ (2023–24 LUMS study). — [Dawn](https://www.dawn.com/news/1616450); [QSR Media](https://qsrmedia.com/food-services/news/foodpanda-injects-12b-pakistans-economy-in-2023-2024)
- International coffee chains are multiplying in Lahore, Karachi and Islamabad, catering to elites and upper-middle-class professionals, alongside traditional chaikhanas. — [Arab News](https://www.arabnews.pk/node/2000021)
- MM Alam Road (Lahore), Clifton and DHA (Karachi), and F-6/F-7 (Islamabad) are described as dense with new cafés (anecdotal blog). — [House of Pakistan blog](https://blog.houseofpakistan.com/pakistan-lifestyle-trends/)
- Chains on Blink: OPTP, Cheezious, Crumble. — [Blink pricing](https://www.blinkco.io/pricing/)

### Inferences
- The addressable segment for a premium ordering + KDS + analytics product is likely urban formal cafés and casual dining in the big three cities, probably a few thousand outlets rather than the 75–90k total. This is an estimate, not sourced.

### Gaps
- No 2023–2026 café count, no growth rate, and no chain versus independent split. No Pakistan Restaurant Association data found.

## Owner complaints about current POS (support, cost, FBR, internet)

### Takeaway
The best-documented pain is FBR integration reliability: sync failures, "fake disconnects", token expiry, and outlets sealed over unverifiable invoices. A Tax Ombudsman blamed substandard software. Mandatory digital payment acceptance (2025) and provincial tax authorities (PRA, SRB, KPRA) add complexity. Outages explain why vendors market offline mode. No restaurant-owner survey on support or cost was found.

### Cited Findings
- SRO 69(I)/2025 (January 2025) makes digital payment facilities mandatory at sales points for POS-integrated retailers. — [Profit](https://profit.pakistantoday.com.pk/2025/01/30/fbr-tightens-tax-enforcement-for-pos-retailers-mandates-digital-payment-integration/)
- The Chainstore Association complained of forced disconnections from profile expiry, failed invoice sync with the FBR portal, unexplained version changes, and store sealing over unverifiable invoices. — [Profit](https://profit.pakistantoday.com.pk/?p=190179)
- The Federal Tax Ombudsman told FBR to stop sealing Tier-1 outlets. It found that sync issues and "fake disconnect" errors were due to substandard software. — [Profit](https://profit.pakistantoday.com.pk/?p=201924); [Business Recorder](https://www.brecorder.com/news/amp/40368868)
- FBR sealed five Islamabad restaurants for issuing fake invoices (date unclear). — [Dawn](https://www.dawn.com/news/1762936)
- PRA runs its own real-time POS integration for Punjab restaurants (PRA manual January 2026). Thresholds and card versus cash rates differ across vendor sources (5%/15%/16% claims), so they are unconfirmed. — [Switcher Techno](https://www.switchertechno.com/pra-pos-integration-punjab/); [Granet Pro](https://www.granetpro.com/pra-pos-integration-punjab.html)
- FBR one-time integration fees of PKR 10,000–20,000 are cited by an older vendor article. Others say PRAL integration is free and the integrator fee is capped. — [OneClickPOS](https://oneclickpos.pk/billing-invoicing-software/pos-system-costs-explained/); [Switcher Techno](https://www.switchertechno.com/fbr-integrated-pos-software-pakistan/)
- Offline mode is marketed by Oscar and Blink, and offline POS by tossdown. — [Oscar](https://oscar.pk/solutions/free-pos); [Blink pricing](https://www.blinkco.io/pricing/)
- 2023 load-shedding complaints in Karachi, Lahore, Peshawar and Quetta (context only). — [Asia News Network](https://asianews.network/?p=104501)

### Inferences
- Any product entering Pakistan must handle FBR and provincial integration, offline-first billing with later sync, and card or wallet acceptance. Without these it is a non-starter for formal restaurants.
- Card-fee competition (Oscar 1.2% + Rs 10) suggests payments are where local vendors make money.

### Gaps
- No owner surveys or review-site data on support quality or cost complaints for specific vendors.

## Kitchen display systems versus printed KOT

### Takeaway
KDS is offered by Oscar, CORN POS (higher plans), OnlineOrder.pk (Golden) and Odoo. Oscar pairs KDS with "automated KOT routing", which suggests printed KOTs remain the norm. No adoption data was found.

### Cited Findings
- Oscar offers a KDS and automated KOT routing. — [Oscar](https://oscar.pk/solutions/free-pos)
- CORN POS includes kitchen display on higher plans; OnlineOrder.pk sends WhatsApp orders to a kitchen display; Odoo includes a kitchen display. — [Menuthere list](https://menuthere.com/best-online-ordering-systems/pakistan)

### Inferences
- KDS is available but tiered as a premium feature. Printed KOT on thermal printers is likely still dominant (unverified). A KDS that also prints a KOT fallback would ease adoption.

### Gaps
- No data on KDS penetration versus printed KOT in Pakistani kitchens.
